These terms cover the Up iOS app, bundle id com.upalarm.app.

The alarm

Up is a wake-up tool. It is not a medical device and it is not a promise that you will wake up. On iOS 26, alarms you allow through AlarmKit use the system alarm volume and can sound in silent mode and Focus. On earlier iOS, and for the notification backups, ordinary notification sounds respect the Ring/Silent switch and Focus unless you allow Time Sensitive notifications. Critical Alerts are not included. Read the reliability note in Settings before you depend on Up for anything that would hurt to miss.

Free and Pro

The free plan is one alarm, the math mission, and the Klaxon sound.

Pro includes a 3-day free trial, then $1.99 per week or $29.99 per year. The yearly plan is about $0.58 per week, which is 71% less than paying $1.99 every week for a year. Pro unlocks more than one alarm, barcode missions, shake missions, step missions, extra sounds, stats, and the optional one emergency snooze. That snooze is off unless you turn it on, and it can be used once per alarm occurrence.

Subscriptions

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed by Apple. After the 3-day trial, the plan you chose renews until you cancel. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends, in Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions. Deleting the app does not cancel the subscription. Prices shown here are US dollars. The App Store may charge the local equivalent.

The auto-renewing subscription is also covered by Apple's Licensed Application End User License Agreement.

Offline

Creating alarms, scanning codes, and finishing missions do not require an account or a network connection. Buying or restoring Pro needs Apple's store.

Your content

Barcode values you scan stay on your device. You are responsible for pointing the camera at an object you are allowed to scan.
