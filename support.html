FAQ

How do I cancel a subscription?

Pro is an auto-renewing subscription billed by Apple. On the iPhone, open Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions, select Up, and cancel. Do it at least 24 hours before the trial or the current period ends, or Apple renews it. Deleting Up does not cancel the subscription. Apple handles the refund rules for that purchase.

What is Emergency stop?

Settings → Emergency stop. Type I AM AWAKE exactly, then stop the alarm. That ends the current ring and the backups already scheduled for it. It is logged in Stats and it does not count as a finished mission or add to the streak. Use it when you are awake and still need the alarm to quit.

Why does it keep ringing?

The mission is the off switch. Up re-rings every minute for up to 30 minutes, and those alarms are scheduled ahead of time. On iOS 26 they are system alarms (alarm volume, through the silent switch and Focus). On an older iPhone they are notifications, which respect silent mode and Focus unless you allow Time Sensitive notifications. After 30 minutes, open Up if the mission is still unfinished and it schedules another burst.

Can I turn an alarm off while it is about to ring?

From five minutes before it fires until the mission is done, edits, the off switch, and delete stay locked. Emergency stop is the way out during that window.

What is free, and what is Pro?

Free is one alarm, the math mission, and the Klaxon sound. Pro is a 3-day free trial, then $1.99 a week or $29.99 a year. Pro adds more alarms, barcode, shake, and step missions, extra sounds, stats, and the optional emergency snooze (off until you arm it).

The alarm was quiet.

On iOS 26, allow Alarms when Up asks. That is the ring that does not depend on media volume. On earlier iOS, allow notifications and Time Sensitive notifications. The silent switch still mutes those notification sounds. This app does not use Critical Alerts.
