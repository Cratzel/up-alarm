Privacy policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Up is an alarm you cannot snooze. This policy covers the Up iOS app, bundle id com.upalarm.app ("Up", "we").

Data that stays on your phone

Up has no account and no backend of its own. These things are stored only on the device:

  • Alarm times, labels, repeat days, sounds, and missions
  • Barcode and QR names, plus the text encoded in codes you scan
  • Wake-up history used for streaks (when you finished, which mission, how long it took)
  • Whether the one emergency snooze is armed, and which occurrence already used it
  • Emergency stops (the time you confirmed you were awake and ended a ring without the mission)

Uninstalling the app deletes this data. We cannot see it.

Camera

The camera is used to scan a barcode or QR code you point it at, either to register it or to finish a mission. Frames are not saved as photos and are not uploaded.

Motion

Shake and step missions read the motion sensors while that screen is open. Up stores that the mission finished. It does not keep a step-by-step log.

Notifications and alarms

Alarms are scheduled on the device with local notifications and, on iOS 26 when you allow it, Apple's AlarmKit. Up does not operate a push-notification server.

Purchases

If you start a subscription, Apple processes the payment. When this build includes a RevenueCat key, the app sends RevenueCat an anonymous app user id and the purchase receipt so Pro can unlock. We do not receive your name, email, contacts, or precise location. If the key is missing, the purchase SDK is not configured and the app stays on the free plan.

What we do not collect

  • No advertising
  • No analytics SDK
  • No cross-app tracking
  • No sale of personal information
  • No account, and no email sign-in

Children

Up is not directed at children under 13.
